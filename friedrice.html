Fried Rice

Description

Fried rice is a delicious and versatile dish that can be made with a variety of ingredients. It typically consists of cooked rice that is stir-fried with vegetables, protein (such as chicken, shrimp, or tofu), and seasonings like soy sauce and garlic. Fried rice is a popular dish in many cultures and can be enjoyed as a main course or a side dish.

To make fried rice, you can start by cooking the rice and letting it cool. Then, you can heat some oil in a pan or wok and add your choice of protein and vegetables. Once they are cooked, you can add the rice and seasonings, and stir-fry everything together until it is heated through and well combined. Fried rice is a great way to use up leftover rice and can be customized with your favorite ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (e.g., peas, carrots, corn)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil

Directions

  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the mixed vegetables and cook for 2-3 minutes until tender.
  4. Pour in the beaten eggs and scramble until fully cooked.
  5. Add the cooked rice and soy sauce, then stir-fry everything together for 2-3 minutes until well combined.
  6. Drizzle with sesame oil and stir to coat.
