Summer Italian Marinade

Description

This summer Italian marinade is a flavorful blend of olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs that infuses your grilled meats and vegetables with a zesty and aromatic taste. Perfect for marinating chicken, beef, pork, or even vegetables, this marinade is a must-have for your summer cookouts and barbecues.

Simply combine the ingredients, let your food soak up the delicious flavors, and enjoy a burst of Italian-inspired goodness in every bite. Whether you're grilling up a batch of chicken breasts or tossing some vegetables on the grill, this marinade will elevate your dishes to new heights of deliciousness.

Ingredients

  • 1 ¾ cups white cooking wine
  • ½ cup lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon minced onion
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon crushed black peppercorns
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and whisk until well blended. Use immediately or store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.
  2. Marinate your choice of meat, poultry, seafood, or vegetables in the marinade for at least 30 minutes, or up to 24 hours for maximum flavor. Grill or cook as desired.
